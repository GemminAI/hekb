# Primary source: Vincent van Gogh's own letters (not third-party critique)

Source: van Gogh, Vincent. *The Letters of a Post-Impressionist: Being the
Familiar Correspondence of Vincent van Gogh*, section "Letters to His
Brother" (Theo van Gogh). Project Gutenberg EBook #40393
(https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/40393), public domain.

This is a **primary source** -- the artist's own words, describing this
exact picture while working on it -- not a third-party critique. It is
included because no real public-domain third-party critical text specific
to this work was found (see report.md for the search that was performed).

Retrieved: 2026-08-04

...I will simply paint my bedroom. This time the colour shall do
everything. By means of its simplicity it shall lend things a grand
style, and shall suggest absolute peace and slumber to the spectator. In
short, the mere sight of the picture should be restful to the spirit, or
better still, to the imagination. The walls are pale violet, the floor
is covered with red tiles, the wood of the bed and of the chairs is a
warm yellow, the sheets and the pillow are a light yellow-green, the
quilt is scarlet, the window green, the washstand is orange, the
wash-basin is blue, and the doors are mauve. That is all -- there is
nothing more in the room, and the windows are closed. The very
squareness of the furniture should intensify the impression of rest. As
there is no white in the picture, the frame should be white.
