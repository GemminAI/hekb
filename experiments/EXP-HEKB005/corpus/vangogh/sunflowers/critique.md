# Primary source: Vincent van Gogh's own letters (not third-party critique)

Source: van Gogh, Vincent. *The Letters of a Post-Impressionist: Being the
Familiar Correspondence of Vincent van Gogh*, section "Letters to His
Brother" (Theo van Gogh). Project Gutenberg EBook #40393
(https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/40393), public domain.

This is a **primary source** -- the artist's own words, describing this
series while painting it -- not a third-party critique. It is included
because no real public-domain third-party critical text specific to this
work was found (see report.md for the search that was performed).

Retrieved: 2026-08-04

---

I am now painting here with as much enthusiasm as the man of Marseilles
eats his bouillabaisse, and this will not surprise you seeing that my
subject consists of sunflowers. I have three pictures in progress: (1)
Large flowers in a green vase; (2) Three flowers, two in the bud and one
in bloom, on a royal blue ground; (3) Twelve flowers and buds in a
yellow vase (the latter being light against light), will I hope be the
best of the three. I shall probably not leave it at that. Pending the
time when I shall share my studio with G. [Gauguin], I should like to
decorate it with a scheme consisting only of large sunflowers. ... The
whole scheme is to be a symphony of yellow and blue.
