# Primary source: Vincent van Gogh's own letters (not third-party critique)

Source: van Gogh, Vincent. *The Letters of a Post-Impressionist: Being the
Familiar Correspondence of Vincent van Gogh*, section "Letters to His
Brother" (Theo van Gogh). Project Gutenberg EBook #40393
(https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/40393), public domain.

This is a **primary source** -- the artist's own words, written before he
painted this picture -- not a third-party critique. It is included
because no real public-domain third-party critical text specific to this
work was found (see report.md for the search that was performed).

Retrieved: 2026-08-04

I must also paint a starry night, with cypresses, or, perhaps, over a
field of ripe corn. We get wonderful nights here. I am possessed by an
insatiable lust for work. I shall be glad to see the result at the end
of the year.
